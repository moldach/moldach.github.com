A useful tool still needs people who know when to use it, when to question it and what to do when it fails. I help small teams turn an AI or automation capability into a documented working practice, using their actual workflow rather than a generic prompt demonstration.

## When adoption needs attention

This is for teams that have a working tool, a pilot or a clear proposed workflow but lack a shared way to use it. Different answers, unclear responsibility, forgotten handoffs and a dependence on one technically confident person are practical issues to address.

If the underlying system is not ready, we identify that before treating training as the answer. [Focused implementation](/services/ai-workflow-automation/) may be the next step instead.

## What we develop together

- Role-specific examples and a practical walkthrough of the agreed workflow.
- Plain-language operating instructions and a checklist for reviewing outputs.
- Clear boundaries for business information, approvals and escalation.
- A handover identifying the workflow owner, account access and maintenance responsibilities.
- A short feedback and improvement process, with a way to report problems.

The material is specific to the tools and work we agree to cover. It is not a certification programme or a guarantee of a particular productivity gain.

## How remote sessions work

We establish the participants' starting point, then work through representative examples by screen share. Participants practise the task and explain how they would recognise a result that needs checking. Documentation supports the session so it remains useful afterwards.

Recordings are optional and require agreement from everyone involved. Real business material is not reused in public training examples without clearance.

## Human judgment stays explicit

Training includes limitations, not only successful demonstrations. We cover incomplete inputs, unreliable answers, tool failures and decisions that remain with a person. The team should know who to contact, how to pause a workflow and which changes require another review.

The number of roles, workflows, sessions and documentation needs determines the scope. We also agree whether follow-up support or revisions are included. A written proposal sets those expectations; no course price, completion badge or outcome statistic is invented here.

## Questions teams ask

### Do participants need programming experience?

We tailor the working examples to the people using the system. Technical implementation and day-to-day operation are different responsibilities and can need different sessions.

### Can the training use our existing workflow?

Yes, subject to agreed access and information-handling boundaries. If it is unclear which workflow is useful, begin with an [opportunity assessment](/services/ai-opportunity-assessment/).
