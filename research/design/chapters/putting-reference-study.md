# PuttPulse putting-area reference study

Reviewed 2026-10-22 after Matthew corrected the initial fairway/tee-pad imagery.

## Sources inspected

- [Olympus course feature and photographs, UDisc](https://udisc.com/blog/post/olympus-disc-golf-worlds-best-disc-golf-courses). The inspected basket image is an attributed photograph of a temporary basket at the 2020 Throw Down the Mountain event. It informs basket anatomy and a wooded course setting, not a claim about the exact current Olympus putting area.
- [Olympus course directory, PDGA](https://www.pdga.com/course-directory/course/olympus). Confirms the Brooksville course identity and target type.
- [PDGA Major Course Marking Standards](https://www.pdga.com/pdga-documents/pdga-major-events/pdga-major-course-marking-standards). The inspected Putting Areas section identifies orange marking whiskers for Circle 1, with twelve markers on a 10-meter radius. This informs the appearance of a marked putting circle; it does not imply that every practice green or Olympus hole is identically prepared.
- [Presco manufacturer catalog](https://presco.com/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/Presco-Catalog.pdf). Describes flexible plastic whiskers attached to buried fasteners, rather than cones or tall flags.
- [Marking-whisker photographs, EngineerSupply](https://www.engineersupply.com/the-value-of-presco-marking-whiskers.aspx). Inspected actual filament bundles and fasteners through native Chrome.
- [Blue Ribbon Pines course photographs, UDisc](https://udisc.com/courses/blue-ribbon-pines-aCxV/photos). Inspected one photograph of a blue basket on red wood-chip mulch. This is a separate course reference, not an Olympus image.

The browser connector blocked the Olympus image host; native Chrome successfully displayed the public image. Screenshots of inspected photographs stay in the private planning workspace because browser chrome contains unrelated tabs. No third-party reference photo is licensed or copied into the production asset collection by this study. Generated illustrations are original stylizations, not photographs of a specific course.

## Visual requirements

- One normal-proportioned chain basket at the center of the composition.
- Basket sits on a modest, slightly raised mound of reddish-brown wood-chip mulch, with a shallow natural edge. No huge podium, steps, concrete slab or artificial tee mat.
- The central mulch bed is much smaller than the surrounding grassy putting circle.
- Small orange plastic whisker tufts define the circle; fasteners are buried. Markers are not golf balls, tee markers, tall stakes, cones, flags or a painted solid ring.
- Use a putting-area camera composition. No tee pad, launch area or long fairway corridor anywhere in the vignette.
- Surrounding trees are background context. Preserve the selected premium-paper art direction without sacrificing recognizable basket/marker anatomy.
- Apply the same corrected asset language to the homepage thumbnail, Work chapter and About miniature. Preserve the required footer face interaction.
