## Implementation behavior

Use bounded per-object animation rather than the same motion on every page. Pause offscreen video; provide pause, poster and explicit playback under reduced motion. Caption spoken media. Separate source captures from newly recreated WebGL demonstrations and label them accordingly.

All six paper tabs become selectable controls. Hover/focus can lift a tab slightly; activation turns the book and synchronizes navigation. Paper folding, video decoding, raycasting and face deformation need browser and physical-device validation before performance claims.
