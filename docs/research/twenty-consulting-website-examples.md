# Twenty Website References for an AI Consulting Practice

Reviewed on 22 October 2026.

The site should establish Matthew as a practical AI and agent consultant for small businesses while also making his experience, judgment, and delivery ability easy for full-time hiring teams to assess. Preserve the blog and connect it to work examples.

These are established public references spanning individual experts, technical publications, and larger consultancies. They are not a traffic-ranked top twenty or twenty direct small-business competitors. The recommendations below are design interpretations based on current homepage content. Desktop screenshots were also inspected for thoughtbot, Eugene Yan, Hamel Husain, Brittany Chiang, Section AI, swyx, and Maggie Appleton. Other entries are structural references from extracted site content, not complete visual or interaction audits.

## Review first

Start with thoughtbot for service structure, Eugene Yan for personal expertise, Brittany Chiang for employer navigation, Section for business-value messaging, and swyx or Maggie Appleton for personality and editorial character. Hamel is a strong additional reference for making a specific AI specialty credible.

## Recommended direction

Build a personal consulting practice with a strong editorial layer. The homepage should explain who you help and the practical improvement you deliver, then show selected work and useful writing. Keep an obvious path to your experience and resume.

Possible opening statement to refine:

> I help small businesses put AI to work — through useful automations, dependable agents, and teams that know how to use them.

Use a main invitation such as **Discuss a business challenge** and a second route such as **Explore my work**. An Experience page can serve hiring teams directly without making the main page read like an availability announcement.

## Suggested navigation

**Home · Services · Work · Writing · About · Contact**

Place the resume and a concise experience summary within About, or promote Experience into navigation if interviewing becomes the immediate priority.

## Suggested homepage order

1. Clear offer, personal introduction, and contact invitation.
2. Three understandable services: discover opportunities, implement a focused workflow, train and support the team.
3. Two or three selected case studies.
4. Short explanation of how an engagement works.
5. Selected writing that answers actual business questions.
6. Background and experience.
7. Contact invitation.

For each case study, distinguish the initial problem, your contribution, system or workflow delivered, observed outcome, and lessons. Label demonstrations, estimates, and measured client results accurately.
