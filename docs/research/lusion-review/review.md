## Updated creative direction

Use a deliberate combination of video, interactive WebGL, and readable HTML content. Video should be part of the original art direction rather than added at the end.

| Surface | Proposed treatment | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | An original short cinematic sequence in which programming fragments assemble into a useful agent workflow | Excite visitors and communicate the consulting offer |
| Pop-up book | Interactive WebGL page turns, paper geometry, camera movement, and selectable chapters | Give visitors a memorable way to explore |
| Work | Short video demonstrations and process walkthroughs inside clear case studies | Show delivered behavior and personal contribution |
| Chapter scenes | Restrained loops using the same paper materials and lighting as the book | Maintain visual continuity |
| Writing | Readable article pages with demonstrations where they improve understanding | Preserve the blog as a substantial resource |
| Footer portrait | Required interactive face deformation using the supplied headshot, with Reset and Close | Add a personal discovery after the main content |

Retain direct navigation and a contact route while motion plays. Default to silent video with explicit sound controls; use poster images, pause controls, reduced-motion alternatives, and simpler mobile scenes. These are proposed implementation requirements, not tested properties of Lusion.

## Design interpretation

The useful lesson is the contrast between visually complex moving scenes and a calm, legible interface. For Matthew, keep thoughtbot's service structure, Every's editorial quality, Metalab's project emphasis, and Section's clear layout. Develop original paper-based imagery with programming and agent workflows as the central subject. Personal interests can appear through mountain terrain, tabletop strategy, and disc-flight motifs where they strengthen the story.
