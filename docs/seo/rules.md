# SEO implementation rules

These rules apply to all content, routes, graphics and deployment changes. The audience is international remote small-business clients; preserve clear engineering evidence for hiring teams.

1. **HTML first.** Generate complete primary content and real anchors before JavaScript. Book navigation, project hotspots and media enhance that HTML. Verify navigation and reading with scripts disabled.
2. **One clear intent per page.** Put the offer or answer near the beginning, use descriptive headings and contextual links. Write to help the reader; do not enforce keyword densities, fixed paragraph lengths or arbitrary word counts.
3. **Evidence before claims.** Record Matthew's contribution, original publication dates, source attribution and project limitations. Publish measured outcomes only with evidence. Historical visualization work is not proof of deployed business agents.
4. **One approved production origin.** Public launch requires an owned domain supplied by Matthew. Derive canonical, social, structured-data and sitemap URLs from it. Reject missing, placeholder, localhost, reserved test and provider-preview origins in production. Previews are non-indexable and generate no public sitemap entries.
5. **Useful navigation.** Each chapter and featured Writing exhibit has an HTML destination as well as its eventual mesh hotspot. Direct links, keyboard/touch access and browser Back must work. Optional model controls do not replace article links.
6. **Honest metadata.** Use unique useful titles/descriptions, a clear page heading and appropriate Person/ProfilePage, Article/BlogPosting, Service and BreadcrumbList data matching visible content. No invented ratings, reviews, addresses, prices or credentials. Schema validity is separate from Google feature eligibility.
7. **Publish deliberately.** Drafts, future content, templates, internal search and preview builds do not enter production indexing. Use validated dates and stable slugs. Latest writing sorts by original publication date, independently of featured picks.
8. **Protect reading performance.** Reserve image/media dimensions, serve responsive optimized images, and load graphics/portrait/media progressively. Measure LCP ≤2.5 s, CLS ≤0.1 and INP ≤200 ms as targets, separating lab results from field evidence.
9. **Preserve links during migration.** Retain useful articles, slides, downloads and attribution. Verify destination-specific redirects and genuine 404s. A static forwarding page is not an HTTP permanent redirect. Preserve the old published tree until approved cutover.
10. **Measure actual discovery.** Use owner-verified Search Console for the approved origin and inspect representative pages after deployment. Record baseline dates and relevant service enquiries; do not invent rankings, search volume or traffic forecasts.
11. **No ranking shortcuts.** Helpful FAQs are editorial content, not a retired FAQ-rich-result strategy. Special AI files/schema and fixed answer lengths are not requirements for Google AI features. Tools and hosting changes do not guarantee ranking.
