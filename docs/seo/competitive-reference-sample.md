# Initial service-search reference sample

22 October 2026. Exploratory web searches for small-business AI consulting/workflow automation and AI-agent implementation surfaced these five public provider pages; their own page content was opened. This is a qualitative positioning sample, not a verified top-five Google result set, backlink audit, market-share estimate or endorsement. No rank, search volume, keyword difficulty or domain-authority figures were obtained.

| Provider's own page | Observed positioning | Implication for Matthew's content |
|---|---|---|
| [Patwon](https://www.patwon.com/) | Plain-language small-business AI help, assessment, implementation and workforce training; remote US service described. | Name the buyer and engagements plainly; Matthew should describe his actual international availability rather than copy another provider's geography or commercial terms. |
| [Bradley Hankins](https://www.bradleyhankins.com/remote-ai-workflow-consultant.html) | Dedicated explanation of remote workflow assessment and implementation. | Explain how discovery, collaboration and handover work remotely, including practical client inputs. |
| [Ignitara](https://ignitara.com/) | Owner-run businesses, collaborative working sessions and systems the customer owns. | Clarify ownership, documentation and team learning using Matthew's actual offer. |
| [Palmetto Group AI](https://palmettogroup.ai/services/implementation/) | Assessment, tool selection, implementation and adoption are explicit service stages. | Distinguish the deliverables of the three existing engagement types. Do not borrow their durations or outcome claims. |
| [Right Flow AI](https://rightflowai.com/services/ai-consulting) | Workflow-first assessment, choosing AI or ordinary automation, followed by implementation. | Demonstrate engineering judgment about when AI is appropriate; avoid vague AI excitement without concrete scope. |

Inference: the premium book can differentiate the presentation, while clear offer/process/evidence content must carry the service-search intent. This sample alone does not establish an underserved keyword or prove demand. A geographic/search-volume study and actual Search Console baselines remain unperformed.
